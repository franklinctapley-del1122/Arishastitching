# Arisha Homepage Visual Redesign Brief

## Source of truth
This brief follows `docs/MASTER-GLOBAL-RULES.md` and the supplied semantic content framework and ChatGPT leads case study. The Local SEO Audit Workbook itself was not found in the accessible project files, so its full sheet-by-sheet checklist must not be represented as reviewed.

## 1. Page identity
- URL: `/`
- Page role: Core commercial homepage
- Entity: Arisha Ladies Stitching & Boutique
- Audience: Women in Lahore looking for ladies suit stitching, party wear, bridal wear, alterations and clear starting prices
- Primary intent: Find a ladies tailor in Lahore / Pak-Arab Housing Scheme and enquire or request an appointment
- Conversion: WhatsApp enquiry, appointment request, directions to the single shop

## 2. Query and entity coverage
Keep the homepage focused on ladies tailor in Lahore, Pak-Arab Housing Scheme, custom ladies stitching, suit stitching, party wear, bridal wear, alterations, indicative prices, measurements, appointment requests and Lahore delivery. Service pages retain their own specific intent. Do not turn the homepage into a duplicate of service pages.

## 3. Attribute filtration
Prioritise only verified and conversion-relevant attributes: one real shop location, female-owned business, operating since 2021, services actually offered, indicative PKR starting prices, measurement options, appointment confirmation, collection/delivery arrangements and WhatsApp contact. Do not add unsupported claims, opening hours, reviews, guarantees or service promises.

## 4. Contextual vector
- H1: Ladies Tailor in Lahore for Custom Stitching
- H2 groups: services and occasions; transparent starting rates; how ordering and measurements work; real shop location and confirmed Lahore delivery areas; practical FAQs
- H3s: service-specific details, price clarifications, measurement options, order timing confirmation and delivery/collection details

## 5. Contextual structure and visual system
Create a luxury-modern Lahore boutique feel while preserving the approved maroon, gold and cream identity:
- Deep oxblood / plum as the anchor, warm ivory as the main canvas, muted champagne gold for accents, restrained dusty-rose/taupe supporting tones.
- Editorial serif display typography paired with a clean readable sans-serif.
- Strong editorial hero with one relevant Pakistani outfit photograph, generous whitespace and clear appointment/WhatsApp actions.
- Consistent image-led service cards with distinct images per category; no repeated casual image across unrelated services.
- Rich fabric/embroidery detail imagery as supporting visuals; label stock imagery as inspiration, never as Arisha's completed work.
- Restrained borders, refined image crops, subtle shadows, clear spacing and mobile-first responsive layout.
- Keep text and business facts in crawlable HTML, not embedded in images.

## 6. Contextual bridges
Homepage links to /services/, /stitching/, /party-wear/, /bridal-wear/, /casual-wear/, /western-wear/, /alteration/, /rate-list/, /book-appointment/, /location/ and relevant supporting guides. Use descriptive anchors and preserve the current URL architecture.

## 7. Uniqueness and quality additions
Use the actual Pak-Arab shop context, confirmed prices, measurement choices, honest appointment-request process, Lahore delivery clarification and real WhatsApp contact. Do not invent testimonials or portray stock photos as actual customer work.

## Acceptance checks
- Existing routes, forms, calculator, navigation, WhatsApp links and appointment request behaviour remain functional.
- No inaccurate business facts, prices, branches, reviews or promises.
- All image alt text describes the actual image and identifies inspiration imagery appropriately.
- Check 360px, 390px, tablet and desktop layouts, keyboard focus, contrast and reduced-motion behaviour.
- Verify production build, pre-rendered HTML, canonical/title/description, internal links, sitemap and live Vercel deployment.
