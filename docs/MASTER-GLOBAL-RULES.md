## 6. SEO strategy

Every page must have one defined purpose, primary search intent, audience, main entity, conversion goal and unique contribution. Research search results and competitors before significant content planning. Do not invent keyword volumes, difficulty scores or rankings; use actual keyword-tool data for numerical claims.

Core local intent:
- Homepage: ladies tailor in Lahore, with strong accurate relevance to Pak-Arab Housing Scheme / Ferozpur Road.
- Service pages: their own specific stitching service intent.
- Location page: one real shop address, directions and accurate service areas.
- Blog: useful support for outfit planning, measurements, fabrics, fit, finishing and related services.

Prevent keyword cannibalisation. Do not make multiple pages compete for the same intent without a clear reason. Use service-area terms naturally and do not imply a physical branch in delivery areas.

## 7. Mandatory seven-part content brief

Before writing or substantially revising an important page, prepare:
1. **Page Identity Block:** purpose, intent, audience, entity, primary query and conversion objective.
2. **Query Network:** primary/supporting queries, questions, intent variations and related entities.
3. **Attribute Filtration (PPR):** identify and prioritise attributes based on research and user needs; never invent weighting percentages.
4. **Contextual Vector:** logical H1 main context, H2 major contexts and H3 supporting details.
5. **Contextual Structure:** appropriate content format, direct answers, useful explanations and practical detail.
6. **Contextual Bridges:** exact internal target URLs, anchor text, placement and relevant upward/lateral/downward links.
7. **Uniqueness Additions:** real business facts, original images, accurate prices, genuine process details and useful competitor gaps.

Research before headings. Do not force arbitrary word counts or headings.

## 8. Content quality, AEO and GEO

Write natural, clear, helpful English. Answer the visitor's actual question directly. Avoid filler, generic AI introductions, repetition and keyword stuffing. Use descriptive headings, concise answer passages, consistent entity facts, crawlable HTML and relevant structured data. Content should be easy for search engines and AI systems to understand and quote. Never invent expertise or business facts.

## 9. Internal linking

Plan links in every important content brief. Link homepage to key services; service pages to relevant related services, rates and booking; blog guides to relevant services and tools; location/contact pages to directions, appointment and WhatsApp. Specify exact URL, natural anchor and placement. Avoid orphan pages, unrelated links and repetitive exact-match anchors.

## 10. Local SEO and structured data

Keep business name, phone, one physical address, official Maps URL and service-area facts consistent across site metadata, visible content, footer, contact page and schema. Use appropriate Schema.org types such as Tailor/LocalBusiness, PostalAddress, areaServed, BreadcrumbList and BlogPosting where applicable. Use only accurate properties. Add opening hours only after confirmation. Never create multiple LocalBusiness entities for delivery areas. Never add unsupported review or aggregate-rating schema.

## 11. Technical SEO

Verify HTTPS, canonical URLs, indexability, robots directives, sitemap validity, robots.txt, URL structure, HTTP status codes, 404 behaviour, redirects, internal links, mobile rendering, page speed, image optimisation, metadata, social metadata and structured data. Do not assume a successful React build means all routes are crawlable or indexed. Check rendered HTML and crawlability, including the limitations of client-side rendering. Sitemap lastmod must be omitted unless reliable page-specific modification dates exist; never set every URL's lastmod to build date.

## 12. UX, accessibility and conversion

Prioritise mobile users. Check responsive navigation, readable typography, spacing, no horizontal overflow, tap targets, accessible labels, form validation and keyboard usability. Primary conversions: WhatsApp enquiry, appointment request, phone contact and directions to the one shop. Make it clear that appointment requests require confirmation.

## 13. Visual and image standards

Maintain the established maroon, gold and cream boutique identity, with elegant readable typography. Prefer genuine business/work photography when available and authorised. Use accurate alt text, suitable dimensions and compressed assets. Clearly identify inspiration images. Never imply stock images show completed customer work.
